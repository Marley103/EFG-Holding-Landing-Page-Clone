Our Services Overview

Operating within three distinct verticals — the Investment Bank (EFG Hermes), Non-Bank Financial Institutions (NBFI) (EFG Finance), and Commercial Bank (BANK NXT) — EFG Holding provides a comprehensive range of groundbreaking financial products and services tailored to meet the needs of a diverse clientele, including individual clients and businesses of all sizes.

EFG Hermes, the leading investment bank in the Middle East and North Africa (MENA), offers an extensive array of financial services, encompassing advisory, asset management, securities brokerage, research, and private equity. In its domestic market, EFG Holding serves as a universal bank, with EFG Finance emerging as the fastest-growing NBFI platform, comprising Tanmeyah, EFG Corp-Solutions, Valu, Bedaya, Kaf, PayTabs Egypt, EFG EV Fintech and Fatura. Furthermore, the company delivers commercial banking solutions through BANK NXT, a leading provider of integrated retail, and corporate products in Egypt.

Investment Bank – EFG Hermes

EFG Hermes is the leading financial partner for clients looking for opportunities in MENA. Leveraging a deep knowledge of economies and sectors combined with global expertise, on-the-ground experience in our markets, and a decades-long track record of success, the Firm’s Investment Bank provides individuals, corporations, institutions and governments across the globe with a holistic range of financial services to meet their investment needs.

NBFI - EFG Finance

Through EFG Finance, the Firm brings clients a rapidly expanding and constantly evolving portfolio of non-bank financial institutions (NBFI) including, factoring & leasing, microfinance, mortgage, insurance, payments, micro-VC, B2B marketplace and financial technology company. Innovative solutions cater to the specific needs of a wide array of clients, including individuals, entrepreneurs, large institutions, and SMEs.

Commercial Bank – BANK NXT

In November, 2021, EFG Holding acquired a 51% stake in BANK NXT, marking a strategic entry into the fast-growing and solid Egyptian commercial banking sector enabling the firm to transform into a universal bank. Bank NXT currently offers the full spectrum of retail banking and corporate services. Additionally, BANK NXT provides loan syndication services to companies and institutions looking to finance large national projects.
